0Hangi Tracker — design overview
Direction

Built on the Industry system: steel accent, square hairline frames, "+" registration marks on the one hero card, Barlow Condensed for display type and timer digits (the single Google font), system stack for body text. Industry's accent #5980a6 is under 4.5:1 for button labels, so actions use its 700 step (#416180) in light and 400 (#94bce3) in dark.

Corners stay square per Industry; toggles and radios are the only round shapes. If you want the "rounded, not bubbly" note from the brief, set --r-1 to 4–6 px on buttons and cards in tokens.css.

Readable at 2 metres

Digits are Barlow Condensed Bold with tabular figures at 220 px for seconds and 168 px for m:ss. At 2 m that is about 1.3° of visual angle per digit, roughly 4× a comfortable reading size. Condensed glyphs let the digits fill the 390 px width without shrinking. Phase word at 56 px, set and edge at 26–28 px. Digit contrast is 8.8:1 to 15:1 on every phase (AAA).

Phases without colour

1. The phase word is always on screen. 2. Lightness steps: Hang is the lightest field (L 0.93) with dark digits, Get ready is mid (L 0.76), Rest is dark (L 0.34) with light digits; the inversion between Hang and Rest reads even in greyscale (see row 1d). 3. +10 s is dimmed during hangs. 4. Rest is the only phase with the result buttons. 5. Distinct beep patterns and voice prompts per phase (Settings → Timer).

Assumptions

Sample data: Aliaksei, week 6 of 8, 25 mm, 5 × 5 s, rest 2:30, half crimp, +5 kg, sessions Mon/Thu. "Now" is Tue 13 Oct, 26 h after the last session, so Home shows the 48 h banner. Progress is drawn at full scroll length. Status bars and home indicators are simplified.

5Background graphic — rock face contours

One SVG per theme, stroke only, 3–8 KB each, preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice" so it crops to any zone. Placement: Login — the 300 px hero above the app name, faded at the bottom. Home — a 64 px strip under the status bar, faded at the sides. Nowhere else: Preview, Summary, Progress and Settings are dense, and the Timer never shows it. It never sits under text.

8PWA icon and favicon

The pentagon training ring: five edge slots around the centre hole, hung from a cord in the hang-phase chartreuse; pale steel on deep steel (Industry accent-900). The favicon drops the slots so it stays legible at 16 px. The mark sits inside the central 80% circle, so any maskable crop keeps it whole.
